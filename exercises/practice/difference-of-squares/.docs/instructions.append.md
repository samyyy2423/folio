## In Moth

`square_of_sum(n)` gives the square of the sum of the first `n` whole numbers, `sum_of_squares(n)` the sum of their squares, and `difference_of_squares(n)` the one less the other.

Write it in `difference_of_squares.moth`, and run the tests with `moth test difference_of_squares.test.moth`.
