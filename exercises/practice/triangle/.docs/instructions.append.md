## In Moth

`equilateral(sides)`, `isosceles(sides)` and `scalene(sides)` each take a list of three side lengths and give yes or no. Sides that can't make a triangle (any 0 or less, or one longer than the other two together) give no for all three.

Write it in `triangle.moth`, and run the tests with `moth test triangle.test.moth`.
