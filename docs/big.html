What it's for

Whole numbers of any size, kept as text: "265252859812191058636308480000000". So say shows one, is compares two, and save and json keep them as they are. Each function takes such text, or a whole number, and gives text back: use "big.moth" as big, then big's times(f, 30), big's power(2, 100). As Python's math has them too: gcd, lcm, square_root (math.isqrt), factorial, choices (math.comb), power_mod (pow(a, n, m)), absolute and negated; and in_base and from_base, for bases 2 to 36.
