What it's for

Maths Folio doesn't have built in, written in Folio: random numbers, trigonometry, rounding, roots, logarithms and powers of any number. Any program can have them with use "math.folio", as neural.folio does.

use "math.folio" as math
say math's sqrt(2)                     # 1.4142135623731
say math's sine(math's pi / 6)         # 0.5; cosine and tangent too, of angles in radians
say math's degrees(math's arctangent(1))   # 45; radians(45) the other way
say math's floor(-2.5) " " math's ceiling(-2.5) " " math's round(2.5)   # -3 -2 3
say math's rounded(3.14159)            # 3.142: at most 3 decimals, for saying
say math's power(2, 0.5)               # any power of any number; exp, ln, log10 and log2
say math's absolute(-7) " " math's whole(7.9)   # 7 7
say math's random()                    # from 0 up to 1, the same each run: set seed to change them
say math's between(1, 6)               # from 1 up to 6

math's sqrt(x)

The square root, the double nearest the true one, as a processor's own gives it. x is m times 4^k with m from 1 up to 4, each step exact, so the root is the root of m times 2^k; Newton's method, averaging a guess with m over it, from (m + 1) / 2, is within a step of the root after 6 rounds; and the step is settled by m - r times r, worked out exactly by splitting r in two halves of 26 bits (Dekker's way), against r times a step, by its sign: Folio compares numbers to 15 digits, and a sign is exact.

Inputs

  • x

Written to sqrt with x, at math.folio line 122

Example

use "math.folio" as math
say math's sqrt(2)                     # 1.4142135623731
1.4142135623731

math's ln(x)

The natural logarithm. With x = m·2^k, m from 1/√2 up to √2, ln x = k·ln 2 + ln m, and ln m is 2(z + z³/3 + z⁵/5 + ...) for z = (m - 1) / (m + 1), below 0.18, where 24 terms are plenty.
