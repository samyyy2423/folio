What it's for

PDF files, made in Folio alone: pages of text in the 14 fonts every PDF reader has, lines, boxes and colours, and text wrapped to a width, as those fonts measure it. A page's corner is its bottom left, and lengths are in points, 72 to an inch: an A4 page is 595 wide and 842 high.

use "pdf.folio" as pdf
set doc to pdf's document()
set page to pdf's page(doc)
pdf's text(page, 72, 770, "Invoice 42", "Helvetica-Bold", 24)
pdf's colour(page, "#1a5fb4")
pdf's line(page, 72, 760, 523, 760)
set below to pdf's paragraph(page, 72, 740, 451, "Thanks for your order...", "Times-Roman", 12)
pdf's save(doc, "invoice.pdf")

The fonts are Helvetica, Times-Roman and Courier, each also -Bold, and -Oblique (Helvetica, Courier) or -Italic (Times), and -BoldOblique or -BoldItalic. Text is in their own encoding, which has Western European letters: anything else is written as ?. save() is built with --allow-write; bytes_of() gives the file's bytes.
